FAQ

Questions you should ask.

Does AHX guarantee a token is safe?

No. It surfaces behavioral information, evidence and data limitations, and cannot guarantee that a token is safe or profitable.

Is this a live trading signal?

Not as currently demonstrated. The verified paid response was marked STALE. A freshness audit is required before any low-latency claim.

Must I reveal a wallet seed or private key?

Never. Do not enter wallet secrets into this site, chats, or agent-controlled pages. Review payment details and approve transactions directly in your wallet.
